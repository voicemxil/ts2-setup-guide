# Getting the Game

::: track Legacy
## The Sims 2 Legacy Collection (recommended for new players)

The only version currently sold. Includes the base game and every expansion & stuff pack.

- [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3314070/) <!-- TODO: verify store link -->
- EA App

After installing, continue to [Preparing Your PC](../preparing/).
:::

::: track UC
## The Sims 2 Ultimate Collection

The 2014 Origin giveaway release. **Discontinued — it cannot be purchased.**

- If it's already in your EA App library, you can still download and play it. You are not required to switch to the Legacy Collection.
- Any listing that claims to sell it is a scam (pirated copies or account resale against EA's TOS).
- The Ultimate Collection remains preserved by the community. How you obtain things is your business — this guide only covers setting the game up once you have it.

After installing, continue to [Preparing Your PC](../preparing/).
:::

## Discs / retail

Physical copies work fine on modern PCs but take more manual effort, and are only available secondhand. Complete [Shinki's disc installation guide](https://shinki26.tumblr.com/post/720014266106839040/the-sims-2-disc-installation-and-fix-guide-for) first, then return to this guide for graphics and fixes — the disc install registers itself the same way the Ultimate Collection does, so follow the **Ultimate Collection** steps.

Repacks of the disc version floating around download sites (OldGamesDownload, Mr. DJ, etc.) are not recommended and are prone to issues.

## Mac

- The [Super Collection](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-sims-2-super-collection/id883782620?mt=12) by Aspyr is on the Mac App Store and works on Intel and Apple Silicon. It only includes some of the expansions.
- The PC version can run in a Windows VM (Parallels recommended — [tutorial for Apple Silicon by Natalie Tinker](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MJmavfqgCIo)), but expect graphics quirks.
