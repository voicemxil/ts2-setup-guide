# Preparing Your PC

Three small things before you play. They prevent the most common problems people bring to the help channels.

## 1. Update your graphics drivers

Preinstalled Microsoft drivers from Windows Update can cause issues. Get drivers directly from your GPU manufacturer:

- [NVIDIA](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/geforce/drivers/)
- [AMD](https://www.amd.com/en/support)
- [Intel](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/download-center/home.html)

## 2. Stop OneDrive from syncing Documents

The Sims 2 keeps its saves in `Documents`. If OneDrive backs up your Documents folder, it can cause disk space errors, save corruption, and missing neighborhoods as it syncs during gameplay.

You don't have to remove OneDrive — just exclude Documents from backup:

1. Click the OneDrive cloud icon in the system tray → **Settings**
2. **Sync and backup** → **Manage backup**
3. Turn off backup for **Documents**

## 3. Install an archive manager

You'll need one to extract mods and custom content. We recommend [7-Zip](https://7-zip.org/) — free and open source.

## Recommended specs

TS2 was made for 2004 hardware, so almost any modern PC runs it. For comfort:

- 2 GHz CPU or better, 8 GB RAM
- GPU with DirectX 9 or Vulkan support
- 20 GB free space (~15 GB minimum, plus room for saves and custom content)
- Windows 10 or 11 (Linux works via Steam/Lutris)

[Original 2004 requirements](https://www.pcgamingwiki.com/wiki/The_Sims_2#System_requirements) if you're on older hardware.
